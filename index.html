FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

A modular kitchen is a factory-made, pre-fabricated kitchen system assembled from standard-sized modules or units. Each module serves a specific purpose — base cabinets, wall cabinets, tall units — and can be combined to suit any kitchen shape and size.

Modular kitchen costs depend on size, layout, material choice, and finish. At The Woodverse, our kitchens start from ₹1.8 Lakhs for a basic straight kitchen, and range up to ₹15+ Lakhs for premium German/Italian kitchens with island layouts. We provide transparent, itemized quotations.

Yes — absolutely. The Woodverse owns and operates its own manufacturing facility. Every kitchen cabinet, wardrobe, TV unit, and piece of furniture is manufactured by our skilled craftsmen. This gives us total control over quality, finish, and delivery timelines.

Installation timelines depend on the scope of work. A modular kitchen installation typically takes 2–5 days. A complete home interior project may take 45–90 days from design approval to final handover. We commit to delivery timelines and honour them.

We currently serve Delhi, Gurgaon (Gurugram), Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, and surrounding Delhi NCR areas. If you are outside these areas, please contact us and we'll do our best to accommodate your project.
